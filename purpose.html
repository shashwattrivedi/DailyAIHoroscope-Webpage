WHY THIS SITE EXISTS

A random sentence. Why does it feel like it’s about you?

Every day a language model writes 12 sentences that sound like horoscopes, one for each sign. No planets, no charts, no astrologer. This site is a small experiment: do people still find them true?

THE BARNUM EFFECT

We see ourselves in statements that could describe anyone.

01

Vague on purpose

Horoscopes are written so they can be read many ways, and so apply to almost anybody.

02

We fill in the gaps

If we want to believe, we look for a match between the line and our own day, and usually find one.

03

One hit sticks

A single match feels like proof, and it makes us trust the next one a little more.

Not against astrology

Astrology looks for links between life on Earth and the motion of the sky, built on thousands of years of observation. This site isn’t arguing with it. Believe what you like, just use common sense before depending on anything.

Please don’t follow it

These 12 lines come from a model nobody reviews. If one sounds like an instruction, ignore it. That goes for anything you read online.

What are the dots?

Each sign’s constellation, joined into a figure. There is no single correct way to draw one, a lot like reading a horoscope: we connect points until they look like what we expect. Most people won’t see scales in Libra. The planets on top are where they really are in the sky today.
