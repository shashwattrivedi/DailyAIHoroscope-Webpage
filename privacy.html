Privacy

Daily AI Horoscope is a small, free, non-commercial experiment. There are no accounts, no sign-ups and no ads, and nothing is sold. This page covers dailyaihoroscope.com, its widget, feeds and data files, and its social media accounts and Reddit app.

What we collect

  • Google Analytics counts visits, pages, countries, devices and a few clicks (switching sign, sharing, saving a card), plus how fast the page loaded. It doesn't get your name or email. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland it sets no cookies unless you accept them in the banner; elsewhere it uses its usual cookies. Google’s privacy policy applies to what it collects.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and load times without cookies.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site, keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect it.

What stays in your browser

  • Your cookie choice (analytics-consent), so the banner doesn't ask again.
  • On the widget, the sign you picked (widget-sign).

These never leave your browser. Clearing your site data removes them.

Widget, feeds and data files

The widget, the RSS feeds and the daily data files (see the widget page) set no cookies and run no analytics. The widget’s links only tell this site which website a visit came from.

Social media and Reddit

The daily posts on Instagram, Facebook, Bluesky and Reddit only publish the day’s lines. The site reads public counts (followers, likes, and posts with #DailyAIHoroscopeWasCorrect) to show totals; it doesn't store who liked or posted. The Reddit app only posts the day’s lines to r/DailyAIHoroscope and keeps no data about Reddit users. Each platform’s own privacy policy applies there.
